隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 30 日

我们不收集个人信息

SQL 速学当前版本不要求注册或登录，不包含广告、统计、追踪或推送 SDK，也不会把姓名、联系方式、设备标识、位置、查询内容或学习记录发送给开发者或第三方服务器。

仅存储在设备上的数据

学习完成状态、示例数据库以及您在 SQL 沙盒中创建或修改的数据仅保存在本机应用私有目录。应用已关闭系统云备份和设备迁移备份。卸载应用或在应用中重置沙盒会删除相应本地数据。

权限与网络

应用核心功能不申请相机、麦克风、位置、通讯录、存储等敏感权限，也不需要网络权限。打开本页或支持页时，将由您选择的外部浏览器处理网络访问，浏览器自身的隐私规则可能适用。

儿童隐私

本应用面向一般 SQL 学习者，并非专门面向 13 周岁以下儿童。我们不会主动收集儿童个人信息。

开发者、用户权利与联系

开发者：wmlunar（正式发布后以应用商店展示的实名认证主体为准）。若未来版本加入账号、云同步、分析或其他数据处理能力，本政策会在功能启用前更新并再次告知。

当前没有服务器端个人数据可访问、导出或删除；您可以通过卸载应用或清除应用数据删除全部本地进度与沙盒内容。如需提出隐私问题、更正请求或删除请求，请发送邮件至 learnsql@wmcoder.net。也可使用 LearnSQL 隐私联系入口；公开 Issue 中请勿填写密码、证件号等敏感信息。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

We do not collect personal data

SQL Quick Learn requires no account or login. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or push SDKs, and does not send names, contact details, device identifiers, location, SQL input, or learning progress to the developer or a third-party server.

Data stored only on your device

Lesson-completion status, the sample database, and data you create or modify in the SQL sandbox remain in the app's private on-device storage. Cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. Uninstalling the app or resetting the sandbox removes the corresponding local data.

Permissions and network access

The core app requests no camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or other sensitive permission, and requires no internet permission. When you open this policy or the support page, your chosen external browser handles that network request under its own privacy terms.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for general SQL learners and is not primarily directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal data.

Developer, deletion, and contact

Developer: WM Coder / wmlunar (the verified legal developer identity will appear on the app-store listing). There is no server-side personal data to access, export, or delete. You can remove all local progress and sandbox data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

For privacy, correction, or deletion questions, email learnsql@wmcoder.net. You may also use the LearnSQL privacy contact form; do not post passwords, identity documents, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
