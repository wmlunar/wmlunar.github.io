支持与帮助

SQL 执行结果异常

请确认语句使用 SQLite 方言。可以在“SQL 沙盒”右上角选择重置，恢复内置示例数据库。

学习进度

进度只保存在当前设备。卸载应用或清除应用数据后无法恢复。

反馈问题

请发送邮件至 learnsql@wmcoder.net，并附上应用版本、Android 版本、复现步骤和相关 SQL。也可使用 LearnSQL 支持入口；公开 Issue 中请勿填写密码、证件号等敏感信息。

Support

Unexpected SQL results

Make sure the statement uses SQLite syntax. Use Reset in the upper-right corner of the SQL Sandbox to restore the bundled sample database.

Learning progress

Progress is stored only on the current device and cannot be recovered after uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

Report an issue

Email learnsql@wmcoder.net with the app version, Android version, reproduction steps, and relevant SQL. You may also use the LearnSQL support form; do not post passwords, identity documents, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
